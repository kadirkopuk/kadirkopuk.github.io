Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to kdrkpk12@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the level number if it is about a level.

I'm stuck on a level. Use a hint (light bulb), or Undo and try another order. Every level can be solved; the star bar shows how many moves the best solution needs.

I bought No Ads or Premium on a new phone. Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases".

I can't hear any sound on iPhone. The game follows the silent switch. Turn on "Sound in silent mode" in Settings.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Privacy: Privacy Policy. Ad privacy choices are in Settings (where required).
